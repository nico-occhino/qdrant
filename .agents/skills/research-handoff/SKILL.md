---
name: research-handoff
description: Close a research task or transfer a verified revision to the other agent.
---

Locate the existing experiment report or handoff for this task. Update that record at meaningful boundaries. Preserve immutable results and link to them. Use a short in-chat handoff if the task is read-only.
Record branch, full source SHA, worktree ownership and any uncommitted changes. State objective and acceptance criteria, what changed, and completion status. List exact commands executed with outcome and scope; distinguish previously reported results from checks you ran. Include run IDs, artifact locations and relevant checksums.
State verified conclusions, defects, uncertainty and remaining limitations. Finish with one bounded next task and its pass criterion. For independent review give the commit or diff and fixture, then review from a separate checkout.
